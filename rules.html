RULES

The laws that govern Veilrend.

These rules exist to protect the people, atmosphere, and experience of the Veilrend community.

RULE GUIDE

Know the Rules

Veilrend contains several layers of rules covering the community, Minecraft server, bosses, automation, and disciplinary procedures.

01 General Rules 02 Minecraft Rules 03 Boss Rules 04 Automation 05 Banned Items 06 Punishment System

SECTION I

General Rules

The foundation of the Veilrend community. These rules apply to everyone within the server and its associated spaces.

RULE 001

Resolve Personal Issues Off-Server

Personal disputes should be handled privately whenever possible.

Issues between players should primarily be discussed or resolved between the people involved. Staff are here to handle matters concerning the server and its community, not to act as personal mediators for every disagreement.

Staff may intervene when a personal dispute becomes severe, affects other members, or develops into behavior that violates another server rule.

If a situation begins affecting other members, staff may implement measures such as a Do Not Interact (DNI) agreement.

This rule does not override situations involving harassment, discrimination, coordinated targeting, threats, or other violations of the rules.

RULE 002

Keep Sexual Content Out of the Community

Veilrend is 16+ and is not intended to be a space for explicit sexual content.

Avoid overtly sexual comments, descriptions, images, profile content, or other explicit material.

Characters may be described as attractive or beautiful, and occasional jokes are acceptable, but excessive sexual commentary can make other members uncomfortable and is not appropriate for the community.

Keep interactions respectful and appropriate for a 16+ community.

RULE 003

Outside Tea ≠ Inside Tea

Do not bring unrelated outside drama into Veilrend.

Veilrend is a place to enjoy the setting, roleplay, and community. Outside disputes that have nothing to do with the server should remain outside of it.

Venting should also be kept to appropriate spaces. Public channels should remain comfortable for people who are trying to participate and enjoy themselves.

This rule does not prevent staff intervention when another server rule is being violated or when a situation creates a legitimate safety concern.

RULE 004

No Criminal-Related Behavior

Criminal conduct and credible safety concerns are taken seriously.

Veilrend does not permit behavior that presents a serious risk to members of the community.

Serious allegations involving criminal conduct may be investigated when credible evidence or testimony is provided.

Confirmed serious misconduct may result in removal from the community. False accusations made knowingly or maliciously may also result in disciplinary action.

Staff may request evidence and conduct an internal review before taking action.

RULE 005

No Discrimination

Everyone deserves to participate without harassment or discrimination.

Discrimination or hateful behavior based on religion, culture, language, heritage, nationality, ethnicity, or other personal characteristics is not accepted.

Hateful, derogatory, or demeaning speech directed toward members of the community may result in disciplinary action.

RULE 006

Be Sensible & Maintain Proper Conduct

Treat others and the community with basic respect.

Treat others how you would want to be treated. Respect the space, its members, and the time people put into the roleplay.

Harassment, intimidation, pressuring someone to roleplay, forcing another player into participation, and inappropriate behavior are not permitted.

Threats of violence or death are not acceptable, even when presented as a joke.

RULE 007

No Current-Day Politics

Keep modern political conflicts outside the community.

Veilrend is set within a historical-inspired period centered around the 1890s–1910s.

Historical events relevant to the setting may be discussed where appropriate. Current-day political conflicts, campaigns, and contemporary political arguments should remain outside the community.

RULE 008

Do Not Share Personal Information

Protect your privacy and the privacy of others.

Do not share personal information belonging to yourself or another person.

This includes information that could compromise someone's safety, privacy, or identity.

RULE 009

No Generative AI

Veilrend does not permit generative AI to produce community or character artwork and writing.

The server does not permit the use of generative artificial intelligence for server-related artwork, writing, Blockbench models, Figura work, or other creative submissions where original human work is expected.

Examples of prohibited generative AI tools include, but are not limited to:

  • ChatGPT
  • Gauth
  • Microsoft Copilot
  • Other generative AI systems

Tools used for ordinary spelling, accessibility, or technical assistance may be addressed separately by staff depending on how they are used.

RULE 010

Channel Usage

Keep the community organized.

Use the appropriate channels for the activity you are participating in. Advertising, commissions, partnerships, discussions, and other activities should remain in their designated areas.

English is the primary language used in public server channels so that staff can properly moderate conversations and members are not unintentionally excluded.

RULE 011

Artist Safety

Respect the people who create the art and models that bring Veilrend to life.

Do not steal artwork, use another artist's work without permission, or claim another person's creative work as your own.

Artists must not be pressured or coerced into drawing, modeling, animating, or otherwise creating something for another member.

Be respectful toward artists and creators.

RULE 012

Veilrend Is 16+

Members must be at least 16 years old.

Veilrend is a 16+ community. Anyone younger than 16 is not permitted to participate.

Members are expected to provide accurate age information when required by the server's verification process.

Knowingly bypassing the age requirement may result in removal from the community.

RULE 013

Discord Terms of Service

Discord's own rules still apply.

Veilrend operates through Discord and therefore requires all members to follow the Discord Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

SECTION II

Community Rules

Additional expectations for communication, community spaces, and server participation.

01

Be Respectful

Treat everyone with kindness and basic respect. Don't be a jerk.

02

No Harassment or Hate Speech

Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, and personal attacks are prohibited. If someone asks you to stop, respect that request.

03

Communicate Your Boundaries

Members are responsible for communicating boundaries and concerns when they arise. Staff cannot address an issue they do not know about.

04

Do Not Personally Attack Others

Personal attacks may result in a warning, strike, or further disciplinary action. Clear communication is encouraged first.

05

Do Not Incite Drama

If you have a serious issue involving another member, open a community ticket so staff can help address it.

06

Keep It Safe for Work

No explicit or suggestive content in text, images, usernames, or other community spaces. Report serious concerns to staff immediately.

07

Self-Promotion

Unapproved self-promotion is not permitted. Artists may share their information in the designated art commission spaces.

08

No AI Creative Work

AI-generated art, writing, Blockbench models, Figura work, and similar creative submissions are not permitted.

09

No Alternate Accounts

Members may not use alternate Minecraft or Discord accounts to bypass restrictions or disciplinary actions.

10

Community & Hangout Servers

If you wish to create a Veilrend hangout server or a server associated with a town or group, contact the community team through a ticket first.

At least one member of the Veilrend Community Team must be included when required by staff.

11

Know the Rules

Rule updates will be announced through the appropriate announcement or changelog channels. If you are unsure about a rule, ask a staff member.

COMMUNITY

Streaming Rules

Guidelines for members who record or livestream Veilrend.

Members wishing to stream Veilrend should verify themselves through a community ticket.

Verification allows staff to provide the appropriate streamer role and access to the designated streamer spaces.

Before streaming a roleplay scene or hangout, obtain consent from the people participating.

Announce when you begin streaming and when new people enter an ongoing scene.

Keeping documented consent, such as a screenshot, is recommended.

SECTION III

Minecraft Rules

Rules governing gameplay, construction, progression, farms, entities, and server performance.

01

No Griefing

Do not grief towns, structures, dungeons, bosses, spawners, or other protected locations.

If you choose to live in a naturally generated structure, contact staff regarding existing loot or spawners before modifying it.

02

Abandoned Bases

A player's base may be considered abandoned after they have been absent for at least one month.

Items donated to another player by someone who has left should be reported through a community ticket. Items associated with banned players may be reviewed and removed by staff.

03

Do Not Break Spawners or Lootr Chests

Spawners and Lootr chests must remain intact. This helps preserve dungeon progression and ensures players encounter the intended challenge.

04

No Exploiting

Exploiting mods, resource packs, hacked clients, or blacklisted modifications are prohibited.

Examples include X-ray, Freecam, hacked clients, and non-approved versions of minimap software.

05

Cataclysm Structures

Players may not establish residences within approximately 400 blocks of a Cataclysm boss structure.

06

Staff Town Construction

Large or personal constructions measuring 25×25 blocks or larger may not be built within 200 blocks of a staff town without permission.

Contact staff through a ticket if you wish to establish a home, shop, or other structure nearby.

07

No Nether Roof

Building or operating on top of the Nether roof is prohibited.

08

Villager Limits

A player may have up to 8 villagers. Shared settlements have a base limit of 8, with an additional 2 villagers available when town requirements are met.

09

No Wither

Players may not summon the Wither.

10

The End Is Prohibited

The End does not exist within Veilrend's setting and may not be accessed.

11

Do Not Overload Containers

Do not store excessively large quantities of items in a single chest or container.

Extremely large item quantities can create excessive entity data and contribute to server lag or crashes.

12

Play Your Assigned Origin

Players must use the Minecraft origin assigned to their character unless staff approve a change for a valid reason.

13

One Minecraft Account

Players may use only one Minecraft account within the server.

14

Elevators

Elevators may be no larger than 3×3 blocks and should fit the medieval aesthetic of the server.

15

Botany Plants

A maximum of 2 pots may be used per plant. A maximum of one barrel or chest may be placed beneath each pot.

SECTION IV

Boss Rules

Boss encounters are treated as dangerous events within the world and must be approached accordingly.

01

Staff Presence

A staff member must be present when fighting the main Cataclysm bosses.

02

Five Attempts

Players have five attempts to defeat a main boss before they must leave the encounter.

Boss encounters must be treated as an IC event. Repeated deaths should be reflected by the character's condition and roleplay.

03

Wandering Bosses

Wandering bosses such as Frostmaw follow the same five-attempt rule, but do not require a staff member to be present.

04

No /back Abuse

Do not use /back to repeatedly return to a boss encounter after dying or retreating.

05

Identifying a Boss

A boss may generally be identified by features such as a visible health bar, a required summoning item, unique abilities, or a unique weapon drop.

06

Bosses Should Be Feared IC

Bosses are intended to represent serious threats within the roleplay. Gather a party and approach major encounters as dangerous adventures.

07

Locked Boss Structures

Do not loot or fight Cataclysm bosses before they have been officially unlocked.

08

Boss Rule Updates

Refer to the designated boss information channel for current additions, changes, and clarifications.

SECTION V

Automation Rules

Redstone and automated systems must not create excessive server load or remove intended gameplay interaction.

01

No Fully Automated or AFK Farms

Fully automated farms and farms capable of operating while a player is AFK are prohibited.

Small exceptions may apply to Botany plants as described in the Minecraft rules.

02

Semi-Automatic Farms

Semi-automatic farms are permitted when they require active player interaction through a manual activation or on/off system.

03

Redstone Doors & Flying Machines

Redstone doors larger than 5×5 blocks and flying machines are prohibited.

04

No Exploit-Based Redstone

Redstone creations that exploit bugs or unintended game mechanics are prohibited.

Examples include TNT duplication and piston duplication machines.

05

No Infinite Redstone Loops

Continuous redstone loops that operate indefinitely without player interaction are not permitted.

06

No TNT Farms

Farms relying on TNT or excessive explosives are prohibited due to their potential impact on server performance and the world.

SECTION VI

Punishment System

Disciplinary action exists to maintain a safe, fair, and respectful community.

01

Warnings

Warnings are generally used for minor violations and serve as an opportunity to correct the behavior. Repeated violations may escalate into a strike.

02

Strikes

Strikes are formal disciplinary actions recorded against a member for rule violations.

Active strikes remain on record for 30 days. Appeals may be made after that period through a community ticket.

Three active strikes result in a permanent ban.

03

Temporary Bans

Temporary bans remove a member from the server for a specified period.

During a temporary ban, the member may be unwhitelisted and have their server roles removed. Access and roles are restored when the ban concludes, subject to staff procedures.

04

Permanent Bans

A permanent ban removes a member from the server. Bans are generally final, except in rare circumstances at administrative discretion.

05

Immediate Enforcement

Certain severe violations may result in immediate removal without prior strikes.

Examples include serious exploitation of minors, severe harassment, credible threats, doxxing, or behavior that presents a significant risk to the community.

COMMUNITY PROTECTION

Do Not Interact Policy

A Do Not Interact (DNI) agreement requires the involved parties to cease communication and interaction with one another.

This includes direct messages through the server or associated platforms, public mentions or replies, and direct interaction within Minecraft.

This may include trading, collaboration, proximity-based interaction, or other forms of direct contact.

During group roleplay, both parties may be present in the same group when permitted, but their characters must not directly interact or acknowledge one another.

Violating a DNI agreement may result in disciplinary action.

STAFF ACCOUNTABILITY

Staff Are Not Exempt

Staff members are subject to the same rules and disciplinary expectations as other members.

If you believe a staff member has violated a rule or acted improperly, you may open a community ticket and request administrative review.

Staff are expected to avoid favoritism and make decisions based on the available evidence and applicable rules.

VEILREND RULE GUIDE

The World Remembers.

By joining Veilrend, you agree to follow the rules and guidelines established by the community team, administrators, and owner.

Rules may change as Veilrend develops. Updates and changes will be announced through the appropriate channels, and members are responsible for keeping themselves informed.

If you see something that threatens the safety or enjoyment of the community, say something. Report concerns through the appropriate staff channels rather than allowing problems to escalate.

Even when something is not explicitly written as a rule, use common sense and do not deliberately exploit loopholes or create situations that harm the community.
